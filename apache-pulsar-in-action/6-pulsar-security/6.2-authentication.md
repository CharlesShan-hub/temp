## 6.2 Authentication

An authentication service provides a way for a user to confirm their identity by providing some credentials, such as a username and password, to validate who you claim to be. Pulsar supports a pluggable authentication mechanism, which clients can use to authenticate themselves. Currently, Pulsar supports different authentication providers. In this section, we will walk through the steps required to configure both TLS authentication and JWT authentication.
