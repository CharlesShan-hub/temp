## 8.1 Data pipelines

In order to effectively design your Pulsar Functions-based applications, you will want to familiarize yourself with the concepts of Dataflow programming and data pipelines. I will describe these programming models at a high level and point out how Pulsar Functions is a natural fit for this programming style.
